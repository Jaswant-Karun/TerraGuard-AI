## 2. Provenance Architecture & 4-Tier Separation

TerraGuard AI strictly enforces a **4-Tier Provenance Classification** across all user interfaces, map legends, and exported reports to prevent operational confusion between observed measurements and synthetic forecasts:

1. **Observed Measurements (Blue):** Physical sensor readings with documented hardware calibration, timestamps, and known latency.
2. **Verified Incidents (Emerald):** Human-in-the-loop audited events confirmed by emergency dispatchers, municipal engineers, or field supervisors.
3. **Model-Based Estimates (Amber):** Predictive simulations with explicit model version numbers, validation splits, RMSE error bars, and uncertainty metrics.
4. **AI-Generated Summaries (Purple):** Syntheses generated by large language models accompanied by operational disclaimers stating that official municipal orders always supersede automated guidance.

## 3. Caching & Rate-Limit Protection Policies

To guarantee sub-second rendering without exhausting third-party API rate limits or triggering billing spikes:
* **In-Memory LRU Backend Cache:** Open-Meteo weather and OpenAQ air quality responses are cached with a **15-minute sliding window Time-To-Live (TTL)**.
* **Geospatial GeoJSON Vector Cache:** Topological assets (elevation benchmarks, water bodies, critical infrastructure) are cached in MongoDB indexes with spatial bounding queries (`$geoWithin` / `$box`).
* **Frontend On-Demand Lazy Loading:** Vector layers are fetched asynchronously only when enabled by the operator, ensuring initial canvas mount requires $<150\text{KB}$ of JSON telemetry.
* **Negative Response Caching:** Failed requests to third-party endpoints trigger a 60-second backoff with automatic fallback to high-fidelity cached local profiles.
